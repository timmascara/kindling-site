One good question, right on time.

The deck of conversation cards was a great idea. It's also at the bottom of a drawer. Kindling brings the question to the moment instead: soccer pickup, dinner, the drive to practice.

How it works

  • Pick your moments. Weekdays at 6:20. Saturday breakfast. Whenever a conversation could actually happen.
  • The notification is the question. Read it off the lock screen, out loud, eyes on the road.
  • It never repeats. Not even reworded versions of questions you've seen. 900 hand-written questions ship with the app, and it quietly composes more on your phone when those are gone.
  • Swipe right to keep, left to pass. Kept questions live in Favorites, searchable and exportable, forever. Passed ones never return.
  • Kid-safe lock. Every deck stays child-friendly while it's on. Couples and Friends decks unlock for grown-up nights.

Free, then one purchase

The download is free and includes the Family deck with 40 hand-picked questions, plus everything else: your moments, notifications, swiping, favorites, export, and the kid-safe lock. One optional purchase, the Full Deck, unlocks the Kids, Couples, Friends, and This House decks and the questions that never run out. Pay once. No subscription.

Private by design

No account, no server, no tracking, no subscription. Everything stays on your iPhone. Kindling works in airplane mode. Read the privacy policy.
